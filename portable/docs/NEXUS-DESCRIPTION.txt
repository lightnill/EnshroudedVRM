Enshrouded VRM — by lightnill

Use your own VRM avatar as a local cosmetic character replacement. This preview
includes GPU skinning, lit rendering, relaxed hair/clothing motion, menu previews
and held-item alignment. Finger mappings are preferred, with an approximate wrist
fallback when optional finger mappings are absent.

Portable Windows x64 ZIP: extract it, open EnshroudedVRM.exe, select the game and
your own VRM, then launch. No avatars are included. No administrator rights,
installer, updater or telemetry is needed. Only you see the replacement; this
preview does not synchronize avatars to other players. Offline play is the tested
path. The launcher requires the supported Steam build and stops on other versions.

This is an early preview. Very large models, unusual hand shapes, supporting-arm
constraints and untested equipment/animation combinations can need more work.
See the included known issues. No universal FPS/compatibility claim is made.
