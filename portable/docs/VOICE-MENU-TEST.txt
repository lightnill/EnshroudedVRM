EnshroudedVRM 0.2.0 - F8 mod settings

Launch the game with Launch-EnshroudedVRM.cmd, then press F8.
Choose a VRM with Browse or the avatar list. Drag the menu title bar to move it.
Voice to viseme starts Off. Game voice chat requires an active voice session;
Local microphone works offline, with Windows default or a chosen device.
Expand Adjust mouth movement for strength, sensitivity and smoothing.
Appearance presets provides Game lighting/Matte for the whole model and
individual material overrides. Use model preset restores inheritance.
Model and appearance preferences save automatically in State/avatar-settings.json.
Keep State private. Original game appearance remains until a valid VRM activates.
